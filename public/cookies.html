Website guidance

Cookie Policy

This page explains the limited cookie and browser-data context for the public APES Contact Centre website and highlights when separate external services may apply their own cookie policies.

How cookie use works here

This public export is a lightweight route-finding website rather than a logged-in portal or on-site payment system.

Current site behaviour

The public APES Contact Centre uses HTML, CSS and local JavaScript to render route information, filters, release records and the donation popup behaviour. It is designed to work without on-site account areas or embedded payment forms.

First-party tracking on this export

We have not intentionally added advertising scripts or on-site analytics trackers to this public export. Standard browser and hosting behaviour may still involve cached files, network requests and other routine web-delivery processes.

External services

When you open APES helpdesk routes, staff access, other APES websites or the secure Donorbox donation page, those services may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

Managing cookies

  • Use your browser settings if you want to review or clear stored cookie data.
  • Review external-service cookie or privacy notices before submitting information or completing payments.
  • If you disable some browser features, certain linked services may behave differently after you leave this site.
