Before you open a contact form

Public guidance for complaints, welfare, support and governance routes.

Share only the information needed

  • Use the route summary to confirm which APES team receives the enquiry.
  • Include only the details needed to understand your request, concern or complaint.
  • Avoid uploading sensitive documents unless they are necessary for the route you are using.

Complaints, emergencies and privacy

  • Use the complaints route for service concerns, conduct, data protection, governance or finance-related complaints.
  • If a safeguarding issue or animal welfare emergency needs immediate help, call 999 first.
  • Form submissions are handled by the named APES team and should be read alongside the APES Contact Centre privacy policy.
