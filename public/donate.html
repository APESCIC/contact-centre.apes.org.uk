How donations help across APES

Support is shared across public welfare work, governance and frontline services.

APES CIC core operations

  • Helps keep central governance, safeguarding, complaints handling and public support functions available.
  • Supports operational coordination across APES CIC and linked divisions.

Shelter and Rescue

  • Supports rescue response, welfare coordination, rehoming work and practical animal-care needs.
  • Helps APES respond when exotic animals need urgent safe handling and follow-up.

Pet Care Clinic

  • Helps sustain accessible care support, bookings administration and practical welfare guidance.
  • Supports public access to care advice and responsible ownership routes.

Pet Shop, education and public support

  • Helps maintain customer support, education resources and public signposting across APES services.
  • Supports trusted welfare-first communication rather than purely commercial journeys.

What to expect

The donation button opens the secure APES Donorbox page in a popup window where available. If your browser blocks popups, you can continue using the direct secure donation link instead.

Before you donate

  • Check the amount and any recurring option carefully before confirming.
  • Use the secure donation page for payment information rather than sending payment details through general contact forms.
  • Use fundraising support if you need help with sponsorship, giving enquiries or donation-related questions.
